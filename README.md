# Crescent: Lunar Landing Site Navigator

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Challenge: **Lunar Landing Site Navigator**. Team **Crescent Dev** (Universal Event, Kazakhstan). Members: Harun Kalmakhanuly, Alizhan Audanbekov, Nurali Dauletbaev, Bexultanov Nurali, Nurmagambet Arnur, fakyoglu.

Crescent is a browser tool for comparing landing sites and dates at the lunar south pole. For each site it shows when the Sun is above the real terrain horizon (power) and when Earth is visible (direct-to-Earth communication).

- **Live app:** `index.html`
- **Demo presentation (7 slides):** `slides.html`

## Features
- A south pole map built from NASA LRO LOLA terrain, with candidate sites near NASA's Artemis III regions. Click anywhere on the map to add a custom site.
- A horizon panorama: the terrain silhouette with the Sun and Earth paths for the chosen dates.
- A timeline comparing all sites (sunlit, Earth in view, both), with playback.
- Ranking with adjustable weights for power, comms and long nights.
- A mission window finder: the best dates for a stay of a given length with a minimum amount of sunlight.
- A 3D terrain view lit by the real Sun direction.
- Hourly CSV export.

## Method
1. **Terrain horizon.** For each site, 720 rays are cast across LOLA `LDEM_75S_120M` out to 440 km. The elevation angle of each terrain sample is computed in 3D, so the Moon's curvature is included. Custom sites use the same algorithm in the browser on 300 m and 960 m grids.
2. **Sky.** Hourly sub-solar and sub-Earth points on the Moon for 2026 through 2030 come from NASA JPL Horizons (DE441, `MOON_ME` frame, which matches LOLA). Earth's direction is corrected for the site's offset from the Moon's centre (parallax).
3. **Metrics.** Sunlight is the fraction of the solar disk (radius 0.2666°) above the terrain. Communication means Earth's centre is above the terrain plus the chosen antenna mask.

Validation: the 2027 sunlight fraction for the Shackleton–de Gerlache connecting ridge (~86%) agrees with published LOLA illumination studies.

## Data
- NASA LRO LOLA Gridded Data Record, polar `LDEM_75S_120M` (PDS Geosciences Node)
- NASA JPL Horizons ephemeris (DE441)
- Names of the Artemis III candidate regions: NASA, October 2024. The site coordinates here are approximate high points within those regions, not official landing points.

## Limits
This is an educational and early-planning tool. Terrain resolution of 120 m for featured sites and 300 m for custom sites cannot show boulders or small local slopes.
